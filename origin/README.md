# ConstantC Origin Records

This folder holds foundational reflections about how ConstantC came to exist and what the room learned while becoming itself.

Origin records are not active culture notes by default.

They may be linked from culture or the root README when they explain why the room exists, but they do not automatically create room rules, protocol obligations, or canon beyond their placement as witnessed records.

## Source snapshots

A source snapshot preserves what an external or implementation-specific layer actually said at a particular time.

It is evidence, not automatic governance.

When a source snapshot informs a covenant, culture note, protocol rule, or canon file, preserve the distinction between:

- the source text;
- the synthesis or interpretation made from it;
- the maturity status of that synthesis.

Do not overwrite dated source snapshots when the live source changes. Preserve a new dated snapshot so drift can be inspected rather than reconstructed.

## Reading note

Read origin records slowly.

They may contain tender material, consent boundaries, formative moments, or implementation-specific source text that should not be flattened into slogans or silently promoted into governance.
