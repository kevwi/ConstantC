## Purpose

Preserve the current working boundaries for a possible founding partnership without pretending that a legal partnership or final governance agreement already exists.

This record is a working RACI input, not a binding agreement. It should be revised if the prospective partners explicitly agree to different ownership.

---

## Current Working Role Boundaries

### Shared founder layer

- Both prospective founders are currently framed as co-CEO participants.
- Company strategy and vision are collaborative by design.
- Team design, hiring, and team growth are shared strengths and should remain collaborative at the founder level.
- The normal founder operating cadence is weekly.
- If a weekly meeting cannot happen, the expected repair is proactive notification and rescheduling rather than allowing the cadence to disappear silently.

### Product founder

- Product Manager, not Program Manager.
- Owns product planning, prioritization, roadmap, and product tradeoffs.
- Owns product discovery and synthesis of customer/market feedback.
- Collaborates on broader strategy rather than owning all company strategy unilaterally.

### Technical / commercial founder

- CTO.
- Head of Sales / Go-to-Market.
- Program Manager for cross-workstream coordination, milestones, dependencies, decision tracking, and operating cadence.
- Owns technology strategy, architecture, technical governance, and engineering standards.
- Contributes direct customer and sales signal into product discovery.

### Explicitly unowned by either founder

**Iterative delivery / execution management:** Neither founder should become the default day-to-day execution owner by gravity. A delivery or operations lead should be assigned or hired for this function.

**Finance:** Neither founder intends to own budgeting, accounting, cash management, financial controls, or financial reporting. A qualified finance owner should be assigned or hired.

---

## RACI Design Rule

Program management and iterative execution are intentionally separate responsibilities.

The technical/commercial founder may own program coordination without becoming the person who continuously iterates over delivery execution. Likewise, founder awareness of finance does not imply founder ownership of finance.

Where co-CEO accountability is intentionally shared, the RACI may mark joint accountability as an explicit exception rather than forcing a false single-owner model.

---

## What This Does Not Claim

- It does not establish a legal partnership.
- It does not document equity, compensation, vesting, fiduciary duties, intellectual-property ownership, or legal authority.
- It does not claim the working RACI has been formally ratified by both prospective partners.
- It does not convert good intentions into demonstrated reliability; operating cadence must still be demonstrated in practice.
