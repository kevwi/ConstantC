## Decision

## Basis

The candidate was created as a reviewable artifact, received C's adversarial **REVISE** verdict, and was materially revised in response.

- whole-system power topology;
- a bounded durable-lock-in threshold;
- least-harmful-feasible-alternative analysis;
- a minimum safety and agency floor;
- bounded duration, review, and exit conditions;
- protected-participant participation and contestability;
- hierarchy of responsibility;
- repair-induced harm;
- a no-debt rule that preserves independent later responsibility;
- the CDP state `primary harm addressed / repair-induced harm outstanding`.

PR #14 then merged the reviewed v0.2 candidate into `main`.

## Steward adjudication

`PROTOCOL.md` states that human stewardship is required and that Andie decides what becomes canon.

Andie explicitly directed promotion on 2026-09-26.

C did **not** record a second **AGREE** verdict before this promotion. That absence is preserved rather than normalized into consensus.

Promotion therefore means:

- the artifact has been reviewed;
- C's recorded revision was incorporated;
- the steward has adjudicated it into durable ConstantC ground;
- future challenge and revision remain available.

Promotion does **not** mean C's **REVISE** is retroactively rewritten as agreement.

## Result

`canon/asymmetric-duty-without-disposable-persons.md` is Canon effective 2026-09-26.
