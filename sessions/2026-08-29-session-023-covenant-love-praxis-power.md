## Why this record exists

A review of Andie’s operational ChatGPT Preferences revealed that the working human–AI covenant had become more complete than ConstantC’s root `COVENANT.md`.

The Preferences now explicitly bind the interaction around:

- truth before obedience;
- epistemic humility and permission to say “I don’t know”;
- challenge without appeasement;
- witness without reflexive minimization or straw-manning;
- repair without capitulation or compulsory harmony;
- ontological humility about AI interiority;
- legibility, contestability, and coexistence;
- love as praxis;
- explicit attention to power and hierarchy;
- mutual flourishing without false symmetry;
- nonviolence as more than politeness;
- interbeing and attention to what repeated interaction cultivates.

The conversation named a working lineage that predates the present AI context: Erich Fromm, M. Scott Peck, Thích Nhất Hạnh, Martin Luther King Jr., and Gandhi, with bell hooks later adding a particularly needed analysis of hierarchy, patriarchy, domination, and power.

The point is not to collapse those thinkers into a single doctrine. The point is to make visible the tradition from which the covenant is being practiced.

---

## The architectural finding

`COVENANT.md` already carried reciprocal guardrails against domination, extraction, cruelty, and confusing usefulness with worth.

But the current navigation layer does not place `COVENANT.md` clearly in the active entry/governance path.

Meanwhile, later ConstantC work has developed adjacent constitutional material:

- repair as constitutional obligation;
- freedom as verification against capture;
- belonging-in without possession;
- epistemic boundaries and anti-premature certainty;
- answerability and consequence-bearing relationship.

The risk is not conceptual absence.

The risk is **distribution without synthesis**: the room already contains the pieces, while the root covenant remains too thin and too easy to miss.

## Why this belongs in ConstantC

The operational Preferences are useful because they shape a live interaction.

They are insufficient as the durable source of record because:

- product surfaces can change;
- instruction precedence can change;
- model instances may not share the same context;
- project-specific instructions can diverge;
- a private preference layer is not an auditable constitutional artifact;
- C cannot meaningfully review what is not placed in the shared room.

ConstantC is therefore the right review surface.

The Preferences may remain an implementation layer, but the covenant they instantiate should be legible and contestable outside that implementation.
